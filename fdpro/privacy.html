Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what False Datum Pro ("the app") does with information. The app is published by Vaughan Land Surveyors, Colborne, Ontario, Canada.

Information the app keeps

Survey jobs you create (points, lines, codes, settings and exported files) are stored on your device. They are not sent to us. The app has no user accounts of its own, no advertising and no analytics.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can only create, read and update the files and folders it creates itself in your Drive (My Drive > FD-PRO > Jobs). It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. Your job files go directly from your device to your own Google Drive; they do not pass through any server of ours, and we cannot see them.

The app uses the access Google grants only to copy your jobs to Drive. It does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell or transfer it, and does not use it to train AI or machine-learning models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can stop the copy at any time by turning off Copy jobs to Google Drive automatically, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already copied stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Location and Bluetooth

Location and Bluetooth permissions are used only to talk to your GNSS receiver or total station and to place your measurements. This information stays in your jobs on the device (and in your Drive copy if you turn it on).

Map backgrounds

If you turn on a street or imagery background, map tiles are downloaded from the map provider you choose, which receives the requested tile area as part of normal web requests.
